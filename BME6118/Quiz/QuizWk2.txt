Radiology part I: X-rays Quiz
Q1. What wavelength range characterizes X-rays?
A. 400–750 nm
B. 10^-11 to 10^-8 m
C. 10^-6 to 10^-3 m
D. 10^-3 to 1 m
Correct answer: B
Explanation: X-rays are electromagnetic waves with wavelengths from 10^-11 to 10^-8 m.

Q2. Which relation gives X-ray photon energy?
A. E = IR
B. E = mg
C. E = 1/2kx^2
D. E = hf
Correct answer: D
Explanation: Planck relation E = hf defines photon energy from frequency.

Q3. In an X-ray tube, about what percentage becomes X-rays?
A. 99%
B. 50%
C. 1%
D. 10%
Correct answer: C
Explanation: About 99% becomes heat; only about 1% becomes X-rays.

Q4. Bremsstrahlung radiation arises when electrons do what at the anode?
A. Decelerate and emit X-rays
B. Refill inner-shell vacancies
C. Ionize water molecules
D. Annihilate with positrons
Correct answer: A
Explanation: Bremsstrahlung is braking radiation from decelerating electrons.

Q5. Characteristic X-ray energy equals which difference?
A. Incident minus binding energy
B. Outer-shell energy minus inner-shell energy
C. Inner-shell energy minus outer-shell energy
D. Incident electron energy only
Correct answer: B
Explanation: Characteristic photon energy equals outer-shell minus inner-shell energy.

Q6. Why do mammograms use very low kV?
A. To penetrate thick bone
B. To increase tube heat
C. To shorten exposure time
D. To enhance soft-tissue contrast
Correct answer: D
Explanation: Low kV improves contrast in soft breast tissue without bone.

Q7. Which low-energy interaction changes photon direction without energy loss?
A. Photoelectric effect
B. Compton scattering
C. Rayleigh scattering
D. Pair production
Correct answer: C
Explanation: Rayleigh scattering is elastic and changes direction only.

Q8. What is the main source of contrast in X-ray images?
A. Photoelectric effect
B. Rayleigh scattering
C. Pair production
D. Compton scatter only
Correct answer: A
Explanation: Photoelectric absorption is the main source of X-ray image contrast.

Q9. In Compton scattering, the scattered photon has what energy?
A. Equal to incident
B. Greater than incident
C. Zero energy
D. Lower than incident
Correct answer: D
Explanation: Compton scattering transfers energy to an electron, lowering photon energy.

Q10. At 45 keV in bone, which interactions contribute?
A. Rayleigh only
B. Photoelectric and Compton
C. Pair production only
D. No interaction
Correct answer: B
Explanation: At 45 keV, bone shows both photoelectric and Compton contributions.

Q11. Which equation describes X-ray attenuation through thickness d?
A. Iout = Iin + μd
B. Iout = Iin e^μd
C. Iout = Iin e^−μd
D. Iout = Iin/μd
Correct answer: C
Explanation: Transmission follows Iout = Iin e^−μd for linear coefficient μ.

Q12. Mass attenuation coefficient equals linear coefficient divided by what?
A. Density
B. Atomic number
C. Photon energy
D. Thickness
Correct answer: A
Explanation: Mass attenuation coefficient is linear coefficient divided by density.

Q13. Indirect biological X-ray effects mainly involve what?
A. Direct DNA ionization
B. Pair production
C. Heat only
D. Water free radicals
Correct answer: D
Explanation: X-rays ionize water, producing free radicals that damage molecules.

Q14. Stochastic radiation effects are characterized by what?
A. Threshold dose
B. Probability increasing with dose
C. Severity increasing with dose
D. No cancer risk
Correct answer: B
Explanation: Stochastic effects have no assumed threshold; probability rises with dose.

Q15. ALARA stands for what?
A. As low as reasonably achievable
B. All levels are radiation absorbed
C. Avoid all radiation always
D. Amplify low attenuation
Correct answer: A
Explanation: ALARA means keeping radiation dose as low as reasonably achievable.

Q16. An indirect flat-panel detector first converts X-rays into what?
A. Electrical charge directly
B. Sound
C. Visible light
D. Heat
Correct answer: C
Explanation: Scintillator converts X-rays to light before photodiode readout.

Q17. Which material is used as a photoconductor in direct detection?
A. CsI scintillator
B. Photodiode
C. Image intensifier
D. Amorphous selenium
Correct answer: D
Explanation: Amorphous selenium directly converts X-rays into electron-hole pairs.

Q18. DQE compares which quantities?
A. Dose to voltage
B. Output SNR squared to input SNR squared
C. Pixel size to matrix
D. Light to heat
Correct answer: B
Explanation: DQE is output SNR squared divided by input SNR squared.

Q19. Fluoroscopy requires which detector property?
A. Fast readout
B. Very low kV only
C. No contrast agent
D. Single static image
Correct answer: A
Explanation: Dynamic fluoroscopy needs fast readout for multiple images over time.

Q20. Which contrast agent is used for gastrointestinal X-rays?
A. Iodinated intravenous agent
B. Amorphous selenium
C. Barium sulfate
D. Technetium-99m
Correct answer: C
Explanation: Barium sulfate drink is used for esophagus and gastrointestinal imaging.
