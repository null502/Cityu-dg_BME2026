Nuclear Imaging Part I: Applications Quiz
Q1. Which tracer is used for bone repair activity in SPECT?
A. 99mTc-MDP
B. 18F-FDG
C. 68Ga-PSMA-11
D. 123I-ioflupane
Correct answer: A
Explanation: 99mTc-MDP reports mineral turnover and active bone remodeling.

Q2. 99mTc-MDP uptake depends on binding to which bone component?
A. Collagen fibers
B. Hydroxyapatite crystals
C. Mitochondrial membranes
D. Somatostatin receptors
Correct answer: B
Explanation: MDP’s P-C-P backbone binds hydroxyapatite crystals in bone.

Q3. In suspected spondylolysis, what does fused SPECT/CT add to planar bone scan?
A. Glucose metabolism
B. Dopamine transporter binding
C. Anatomical localization of uptake
D. Amyloid plaque burden
Correct answer: C
Explanation: SPECT/CT localizes increased MDP uptake within skeletal anatomy.

Q4. A stress perfusion defect that improves at rest suggests which condition?
A. Fixed scar
B. Normal perfusion
C. Pulmonary embolism
D. Reversible ischemia
Correct answer: D
Explanation: A defect improving at rest supports reversible ischemia.

Q5. Why does 99mTc-MIBI retain in viable myocardial cells?
A. Binds hydroxyapatite
B. Lipophilic cation and membrane potentials
C. Binds PSMA active site
D. Enters sodium-iodide symporter
Correct answer: B
Explanation: Lipophilic cation enters viable cells; membrane potentials support mitochondrial retention.

Q6. Which tracers support PET myocardial perfusion imaging?
A. 82Rb and 13N-ammonia
B. 99mTc-MDP and 18F-NaF
C. 68Ga-DOTATATE and 18F-FDG
D. 123I-ioflupane and 99mTc-ECD
Correct answer: A
Explanation: 82Rb and 13N-ammonia support perfusion PET; dynamic PET can quantify flow.

Q7. Ventilation-perfusion SPECT showing perfusion loss with preserved ventilation suggests?
A. Myocardial ischemia
B. Graves disease
C. Pulmonary embolism
D. Alzheimer's disease
Correct answer: C
Explanation: Q<V mismatch suggests pulmonary embolism from blocked lung blood flow.

Q8. Technegas used for ventilation SPECT is an aerosol of what?
A. 68Ga-PSMA-11
B. 18F-FDG-6-phosphate
C. 123I-ioflupane
D. 99mTc-carbon nanoparticles
Correct answer: D
Explanation: Technegas is made by heating 99mTc in a carbon crucible.

Q9. Brain perfusion SPECT with 99mTc-HMPAO shows reduced perfusion in areas affected by?
A. Stroke
B. Prostate cancer
C. Thyroid nodule
D. Bone fracture
Correct answer: A
Explanation: Brain perfusion SPECT measures blood flow; less perfusion could indicate stroke.

Q10. During an epileptic seizure, ictal SPECT usually shows what in involved regions?
A. Reduced hydroxyapatite
B. Increased blood flow
C. Increased PSMA binding
D. Decreased amyloid plaques
Correct answer: B
Explanation: Ictal SPECT captures increased blood flow in involved brain regions.

Q11. Diffuse increased thyroid uptake on pertechnetate scan most suggests?
A. Autonomous nodule
B. Thyroiditis
C. Graves disease
D. Toxic multinodular goiter
Correct answer: C
Explanation: Diffuse increase suggests Graves disease; check TSH receptor antibodies.

Q12. 123I-ioflupane SPECT (DAT scan) binds which presynaptic target?
A. Somatostatin receptor
B. PSMA
C. Sodium-iodide symporter
D. Dopamine transporter
Correct answer: D
Explanation: It binds presynaptic dopamine transporter, marking dopamine nerve terminals.

Q13. Which renal tracer is retained in cortex and shows cortical defects/scars?
A. 99mTc-DMSA
B. 99mTc-MAG3
C. 99mTc-DTPA
D. 99mTc-MDP
Correct answer: A
Explanation: DMSA shows cortical retention; MAG3 is tubular secretion; DTPA is filtration.

Q14. Why does 18F-FDG-6-phosphate accumulate in cells?
A. It binds hydroxyapatite
B. It cannot proceed further in glycolysis
C. It is excreted by kidneys immediately
D. It binds PSMA active site
Correct answer: B
Explanation: Carbon-2 substitution limits downstream glycolysis, so FDG-6-phosphate accumulates.

Q15. Which is a normal pattern on 18F-FDG PET?
A. High hydroxyapatite uptake
B. High PSMA in salivary glands only
C. High brain cortex uptake
D. No urinary excretion
Correct answer: C
Explanation: Brain cortex normally uses much glucose; kidneys and bladder show urinary excretion.

Q16. In lymphoma response assessment, what does SUV quantify?
A. Bone mineral density
B. Pulmonary ventilation
C. Thyroid hormone level
D. Radiotracer concentration in a region
Correct answer: D
Explanation: SUV standardizes radioactivity concentration by injected dose and weight.

Q17. PSMA is overexpressed on the surface of which cancer cells?
A. Prostate cancer
B. Thyroid cancer
C. Neuroendocrine tumors
D. Lymphoma
Correct answer: A
Explanation: PSMA is a prostate cell surface protease overexpressed in many prostate cancers.

Q18. In 68Ga-PSMA-11, what does the urea moiety do?
A. Binds hydroxyapatite
B. Prevents cleavage by PSMA
C. Coordinates 99mTc
D. Enters sodium-iodide symporter
Correct answer: B
Explanation: Glu-urea-lys motif mimics substrate, but urea makes tracer uncleavable by PSMA.

Q19. 68Ga-DOTATATE targets which receptor on neuroendocrine tumors?
A. Dopamine transporter
B. PSMA
C. Somatostatin receptor SSTR2
D. Sodium-iodide symporter
Correct answer: C
Explanation: Cyclic octreotate targets SSTR2, highly expressed on many neuroendocrine tumors.

Q20. Which 18F-FDG PET pattern supports Alzheimer's disease?
A. Frontal and anterior temporal changes
B. Diffuse thyroid uptake
C. Increased bone remodeling
D. Temporoparietal hypometabolism
Correct answer: D
Explanation: Temporoparietal metabolic changes can support Alzheimer's disease.
