Magnetic Resonance Imaging I: Principles Quiz
Q1. Which statement about ¹H is correct?
A. It has spin zero
B. It has one proton and spin 1/2
C. It has no magnetic moment
D. It gives no NMR signal

Correct answer: B
Explanation: ¹H has one proton, no neutron, and nuclear spin 1/2.

Q2. Which field tips net magnetization in MRI?
A. B₀
B. Gradients
C. B₁
D. Shim field

Correct answer: C
Explanation: B₁ is the transverse RF field that tips magnetization.

Q3. Equilibrium longitudinal magnetization mainly comes from:
A. A slight spin population imbalance
B. Equal spin populations
C. Random transverse phases
D. Receiver coil voltage

Correct answer: A
Explanation: Slightly more spins occupy the lower-energy parallel state.

Q4. At 3.0 T, proton Larmor frequency is about:
A. 42.58 MHz
B. 63.9 MHz
C. 300 MHz
D. 127.7 MHz

Correct answer: D
Explanation: For ¹H, 42.58 MHz/T × 3.0 T ≈ 127.7 MHz.

Q5. During precession, which magnetization component changes direction?
A. Longitudinal
B. Transverse
C. Net M₀
D. B₀

Correct answer: B
Explanation: The transverse vector rotates as the proton precesses.

Q6. Chemical shift arises because Larmor frequency depends on:
A. Receiver bandwidth
B. Slice gradient
C. Local environment
D. Flip angle

Correct answer: C
Explanation: Local electron shielding changes the effective magnetic field.

Q7. Flip angle is proportional to:
A. B₁ amplitude × duration
B. B₀ strength
C. TE
D. Receiver gain

Correct answer: A
Explanation: Flip angle ∝ B₁t; stronger or longer pulses give larger flips.

Q8. A 90° pulse mainly:
A. Inverts M₀ along z
B. Creates T₂* decay
C. Refocuses phase
D. Tips M₀ into transverse plane

Correct answer: D
Explanation: A 90° pulse converts longitudinal magnetization into transverse magnetization.

Q9. T₁ relaxation describes:
A. Loss of transverse coherence
B. Longitudinal recovery
C. Chemical shift
D. Slice selection

Correct answer: B
Explanation: T₁ is spin-lattice relaxation and recovers longitudinal magnetization.

Q10. T₂* includes T₂ decay plus:
A. RF excitation
B. Slice selection
C. B₀ inhomogeneity dephasing
D. Fourier transform

Correct answer: C
Explanation: T₂* is shorter than T₂ due to static field imperfections.

Q11. Receiver coil detects signal from:
A. Rotating transverse magnetization
B. Static longitudinal Mz
C. B₀ shim current
D. Gradient coil current

Correct answer: A
Explanation: Changing flux from rotating transverse magnetization induces voltage.

Q12. Spin echo refocusing uses:
A. 90° pulse
B. Gradient reversal
C. Small flip angle
D. 180° RF pulse

Correct answer: D
Explanation: A 180° pulse reverses phase ordering to form a spin echo.

Q13. T1-weighted spin echo uses:
A. Long TR, long TE
B. Short TR, short TE
C. Long TR, short TE
D. Short TR, long TE

Correct answer: B
Explanation: Short TR emphasizes T₁ recovery; short TE limits T₂ decay.

Q14. Gradient echo is sensitive to:
A. T1 only
B. Proton density only
C. T2*
D. Chemical shift only

Correct answer: C
Explanation: Gradient echo lacks 180° refocusing, so static offsets remain.

Q15. Slice thickness depends on RF bandwidth and:
A. Gradient strength
B. TR
C. Receiver phase
D. Flip angle only

Correct answer: A
Explanation: Δz = BW/(γGz); stronger gradient gives thinner slice.

Q16. Frequency encoding applies a readout gradient during:
A. RF excitation
B. Slice selection
C. Phase encoding
D. Signal recording

Correct answer: D
Explanation: The readout gradient labels x positions by precession frequency.

Q17. One k-space sample describes:
A. One pixel
B. A whole-image phase pattern
C. One slice position
D. One receiver channel

Correct answer: B
Explanation: Each k-space sample contains contributions across the whole slice.

Q18. 2D scan time is approximately:
A. FOV × Δk
B. TE × TR
C. Ny × TR × averages
D. B1 × duration

Correct answer: C
Explanation: One k-space line per TR gives time ≈ Ny × TR × averages.

Q19. Field of view is set by:
A. 1/Δk
B. Δk × TR
C. TE/T2
D. γB₀

Correct answer: A
Explanation: k-space sample spacing determines FOV: FOV = 1/Δk.

Q20. Wrap-around artifact is caused by:
A. Long T1
B. Large flip angle
C. High SNR
D. Small encoded FOV

Correct answer: D
Explanation: Signals outside a small FOV fold into the image.
